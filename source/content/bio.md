Meet Dr. Shaileshh Bojja Venkarakrishnan, an assistant professor in the Computer Science and Engineering department at Ohio State University. Dr. Bojja is broadly interested in designing intelligent data-driven algorithms for current and emerging networking and distributed systems.

**Research Interests:**

- Networking layer of blockchains and large-scale decentralized applications
- Scheduling algorithms for data center networks
- Peer-to-peer networks and information theory

**Education:**

- PhD, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), 2017
- Bachelor's, Indian Institute of Technology Madras, 2012

**Experience:**

- Postdoc at MIT in the networks and mobile systems group
- Internship at Google focusing on congestion control in data center networks
- Recipient of the Joan and Lalit Bahl fellowship at UIUC.
